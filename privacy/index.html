How this company website handles information.

Last updated November 1, 2026

Using this site

This website presents information about Vellbyte, its apps, and its services. It has no account registration, web forms, advertising pixels, or analytics tools configured in the site itself.

The hosting provider may process routine technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. Your browser may also retain ordinary cached files.

Emailing us

When you choose an email link, your email app opens a message addressed to us. We receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Email is not a secure channel for passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details.

Other sites and apps

Some links lead to social networks and other services with their own practices. App support and privacy drafts are available on our support site; each app has its own privacy information, which is still under review.

Questions

Contact contact@vellbyte.com with website privacy questions.
