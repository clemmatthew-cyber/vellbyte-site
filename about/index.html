Vellbyte is an independent app studio building practical tools for everyday life and offering app-building services.

We’re starting with two familiar challenges: keeping shared trip expenses clear and remembering what to pack. Our approach is to focus on the useful details, keep the experience approachable, and make each app easy to understand.
